How It Works

Three simple steps to give the gift of seamless travel.

  1. Choose & Personalize

    Select a preset or enter a custom amount, valid for any business or first class flight. Add the recipient’s details and a heartfelt personal note.

  2. Fast, Secure Checkout

    Complete your payment in seconds with bank-grade encryption. No account or registration needed — your official receipt arrives instantly.

  3. Instant Delivery & Booking

    The digital voucher lands in their inbox right away with a unique code and direct access to a dedicated travel advisor for effortless booking.

Frequently Asked Questions

More answers can be found on the dedicated FAQ page.

A Gift Voucher is a prepaid credit that can be redeemed towards any business or first class fare booked with Business Class.

Choose a value, enter the recipient’s email and your details, and pay securely. The voucher is emailed to the recipient with redemption instructions.

Any remaining balance stays on the voucher and can be used towards a future booking, subject to the voucher’s validity period.

As soon as the recipient receives the voucher email, they can contact an advisor to book a flight using the voucher.

Vouchers are valid for 12 months from the date of purchase.

The voucher is issued and emailed to the recipient’s address immediately after payment is confirmed.
